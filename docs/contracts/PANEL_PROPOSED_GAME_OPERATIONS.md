### Authorization and approval

The permissions below are proposed grants for the named operations. The existing economy read is governed by the accepted analytics authorization policy.

The actor labels describe eligible roles for explicit grants. The server authenticates the actor, resolves the allowed Game scope, and supplies the permission and risk classification. Staff sessions require MFA. Mutations require CSRF protection, a bounded reason code, and the cited case or change reference. The Game owner rechecks actor or issuer authority, target scope, relevant revisions, and execution fences before deciding. Automatic domain workers use the narrowly scoped service identity and carry the originating authorization evidence where applicable.

| Tier | Proposed approval rule |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | Scoped read and audit each page or exact read. Mask sensitive fields by default in every response. Reveal a restricted field only with the operation’s explicit disclosure grant and authorization for that field and subject. No second approver. |
| R2 | Scoped, bounded change with a reason and case or change reference. No second approver. |
| R3 | R2 controls plus requester step-up within five minutes of issuance and dry-run support. No second approver unless a stricter domain rule applies. |
| R4 | R3 controls plus a Game dry run and approval from a second, independently scoped administrator. Bind approval to the exact intent, target, preview digest and expected revisions. Require approval less than 30 minutes old and approver step-up within five minutes of approval. Recheck prerequisites inside the approved execution window. |

These proposed staff rules follow the ADM risk thresholds and timing model. **R4 approval evidence.** At issuance, reissuance and each dispatch, Platform reloads the durable approval and preview and revalidates the exact canonical intent binding, current permission and scope of both staff actors across every affected target, separation of duties, approval freshness and approver step-up at approval. Signing the request attests the issuance checks and binds the approval references to the signed intent. Before handing the envelope to the agent, Platform also verifies that the approval is active and records the dispatch authorization and its revision with the underlying evidence. Deny dispatch if either actor has lost the required permission or scope, the approval is withdrawn or expired, or the checks cannot be verified. The ADM approval object carries only `admin_id`, `approval_id` and `approved_at`. Game verifies the trusted signature, required and distinct approver identity, approval and requester step-up freshness, its own preview digest and target/revision binding, and Game-side runtime eligibility and fences. Approver scope and step-up are Platform attestations under this profile. Any operation whose Game policy requires independent proof of either remains disabled until a versioned evidence profile and verifier are ratified. Native domain wires must ratify their own authorization evidence before use.

**R4 effect authorization.** Before enabling an R4 profile, ratify how Game authenticates a current authorization decision for the exact operation, targets, requester and approver grants, active approval and preview digest. Bind it to the applicable grant and approval revisions. Game checks that decision at the commit boundary for every R4 effect, including an immediate effect or job admission. Serialize grant changes and approval revocation with the Game effect through a shared authority fence. A revocation ordered before the effect blocks the commit, even when the request signature and lifetime still validate. Missing, stale or unverifiable authority fails closed before effect. Record the checked revisions and decision in the effect audit. This applies to native wires through their own ratified profiles. ## 1. Character world or channel transfer

- **Operation ID and authority.** `character.transfer.placement` is an **R4** effect in both variants. For an owner-requested move, the authenticated current owner supplies explicit consent after recent authentication. Platform records a pending transfer intent and applicable paid-service reservation, then hands that exact intent to a different, scoped senior GM with `game.player.transfer.placement`. The GM is the execution requester and performs the Game dry run with fresh staff step-up. A second administrator with the transfer permission and independent scope, distinct from the requesting GM and the consenting account, uses fresh step-up to review the preview and approve the exact intent. For case-bound repair a scoped senior GM originates the same R4 preview and approval workflow with a documented repair decision instead of owner consent where the accepted repair policy permits. Ensure both staff roles cover the source and destination. Game-side owner is Character Authority with the source and destination placement/admission authorities.
- **Request.** Both proposed profiles bind `account_id`, `character_id`, `expected_character_revision`, `expected_lifecycle_revision`, `capacity_reservation_ref`, `transfer_policy_revision` and consent or approved repair reference. World transfer binds distinct `expected_from_world_id` and `target_world_id`. A distinct, versioned `TransferCharacterChannel` profile binds one `world_id` and distinct `expected_from_channel_id` and `target_channel_id` within it. Under the transfer fence, require durable offline status, validate both placements and resolve world-bound obligations before committing the move.
- **Response.** World transfer returns `from_world_id` and resulting `world_id`. The proposed channel receipt returns `world_id`, `from_channel_id` and resulting `channel_id`. Both return `character_revision`, projection epoch/revision and authoritative transfer evidence. One CharacterId and one authoritative placement survive the move.
- **Idempotency and audit.** Map the submission key to one Character Authority `operation_id`. Bind owner consent or approved repair evidence, the GM action, capacity reservation, preview and second approval to one canonical transfer intent digest. Include the operation, account and character, exact placements, expected revisions, policy and service or reservation terms in that intent. Platform verifies consent, approvals and private reservation terms against that digest. Under the negotiated authorization profile, Game verifies the signed authorization and its binding to the exact Game-visible transfer fields, live eligibility, freshness and the execution window. A change to the intent requires new owner consent where applicable and a new preview and approval. Expired consent or approval blocks issuance. Record consent or repair evidence, both staff identities, preview/intent digests, source and destination, obligation disposition and committed placement revision.
- **Paid reservation.** Bind the Platform Wallet charge or one-use entitlement reservation to the transfer operation and canonical intent before the command can become listable. Keep the reservation held while the outcome is missing, ambiguous or under recovery, and reconcile by the original operation ID. On a matching verified current-generation `COMMITTED` receipt, settle the charge or consume the entitlement exactly once. On authoritative terminal no-effect evidence that also fences late execution, release exactly once. Commit the receipt decision and reservation transition together using operation-scoped ledger keys. Timeout, expiry, a projection change or conflicting receipt leaves the reservation held pending reconciliation. Retrying settlement never resubmits the Game move. Keep Wallet and entitlement identifiers and pricing private to Platform. This extends the existing paid-service settlement pattern to the proposed transfer profiles.
