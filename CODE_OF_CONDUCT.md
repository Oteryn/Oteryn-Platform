# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Participants must communicate respectfully, discuss technical decisions on their merits, provide actionable feedback, acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Focus criticism on code, architecture, evidence and outcomes rather than on individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not permitted:

- harassment, threats, discrimination or demeaning language;
- personal attacks, repeated hostility or deliberate disruption;
- publishing private information, credentials or security details without authorization;
- impersonation, manipulation of evidence or knowingly false completion claims;
- spam, off-topic promotion or attempts to bypass repository safety and review controls.

## Scope

This policy applies to Issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, commits, project boards, repository-linked communication and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer through a private channel. When no private contact channel is available, open a minimal public Issue requesting private contact without including sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request changes, restrict participation, lock conversations or block contributors. Responses should be proportionate, documented and focused on protecting project participants and repository integrity.

Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
