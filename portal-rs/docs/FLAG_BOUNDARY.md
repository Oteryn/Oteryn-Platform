# Feature flag boundary

The portal follows the zero-target rule from 2026-11-18. With a feature off,
its routes must be absent or return their documented disabled response, its
work must stay unclaimed, and its tables must receive no feature writes.
Retention and privacy jobs may continue to delete or expire existing data.

The boundary test consumes the route column below. Methods use G for GET, P
for POST, U for PUT, and D for DELETE. A numeric suffix records the exact
disabled response. Unmarked routes must return 404.
`{lang}` expands to `en` and `pl`.
`{prefix}` also covers the unlocalized path. Routes under `/internal/` use
the real mTLS listener, except native admissions and recoveries, which use
their dedicated loopback listener. The dedicated league, bazaar and account
closure listeners must stay closed. Their paths are also probed through the
configured mTLS listener. The test uses a current browser session with a
matching CSRF token and checks database operation counts across the entire
schema, including shared tables. Legacy aliases with 301 must redirect to
their fixed Polish paths. The Bazaar sell fallback accepts only GET. Its
POST returns 405. Native ticket issuance returns its 401 protocol refusal. Native character
lookup returns 503. Internal 503 responses are the disabled guard response.
Browser and API 503 entries cover explicitly unavailable feature surfaces.

## Flag-on checklist

- Complete one full zero-target review round for the feature before switching
  on its flag. Trace public and private routes, queued and scheduled work,
  startup tasks, and direct and cascading table writes.
- Check that every disabled route has a probe and that each disabled job stays
  unclaimed. Check the exact refusal status and verify the database boundary
  on PostgreSQL. For each retention exception, show what expires and confirm
  that the job creates no feature rows.
- Record the reviewed commit, the flag and its prerequisites, the evidence
  from the round, and the resolution of every finding before enabling it.
