## Data, purposes and legal bases

- **Accounts and play.** We process your account ID, email addresses (including a backup address if provided), password hash, account status and language, email verification and recovery records, and records of accepted terms and choices. We also process information associated with your account such as character identifiers, names and game activity shown in the portal. We use these data to register and operate your account, provide portal and game features, and handle account requests under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Public game features may display character names and related activity.
- **Authentication and security.** We process session and token identifiers or hashes, IP addresses, browser summaries, device and session timestamps, CSRF tokens, login and security events, and, where used, protected authenticator secrets, hashed recovery codes, trusted-device records and passkey public credentials and challenge state. Passkey private keys stay with the authenticator. We use these data to sign you in and deliver security features you request under Art. 6(1)(b), and to prevent abuse, investigate incidents and enforce rules based on our legitimate interest in protecting users and the service under Art. 6(1)(f).
- **Support, moderation and audit.** We process ticket subjects, categories and messages, staff notes, reports and enforcement records, and audit records such as actor and target identifiers, timestamps and details of changes. We handle requests to perform the contract under Art. 6(1)(b), comply with legal obligations such as handling statutory complaints under Art. 6(1)(c), and investigate abuse, document administrative actions and establish or defend claims under Art. 6(1)(f).
- **Purchases and billing.** Where the feature is available, we process account and order identifiers, line items, amounts, currency, provider references and transaction states, invoices, refunds and records of coin credits or debits. We use these data to fulfill orders and handle refunds under Art. 6(1)(b), meet tax and accounting obligations under Art. 6(1)(c), and prevent fraud and reconcile disputed transactions under Art. 6(1)(f). The payment provider handles payment instrument details. Portal payment records do not contain full card numbers or card security codes.
- **Consent.** Where we ask for consent for an optional purpose, we use the data for that purpose under Art. 6(1)(a) and record your choice. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal leaves earlier lawful processing unaffected.

Required account or purchase data let us provide the feature you request. Without them we may be unable to open the account, verify access or fulfill the order. We receive data from you and your device, generate records as you use the service, and receive transaction and game records from the relevant systems.

When you register, change your password or reset it, we check whether the password appears in known breaches to protect accounts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If the relevant range is missing from the cache, we send the configured breach-range provider (Have I Been Pwned by default) the first five hexadecimal characters of the password’s SHA-1 hash. The provider receives only that prefix from the hash. We compare the rest of the hash locally and cache the returned range for 30 days.

## Retention

Account and character records are kept while needed to provide the account and handle its closure. Sessions and short-lived challenges have idle or fixed expiry periods as applicable. We keep registered credentials while they are in use and apply the relevant expiration or revocation rules to tokens and trusted devices. Resolved or closed support tickets are subject to a 180-day cleanup window. Financial records are kept for the applicable statutory period. Security logs, audit records and records needed for disputes are retained as long as necessary for investigation, legal obligations or claims, taking applicable limitation periods into account.

## Recipients and international transfers

Authorized staff and providers of hosting, CDN, email delivery and payment services may receive data as needed for their roles. A payment provider may act as an independent controller for its own processing. We may disclose data to competent authorities when legally required. If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we use an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information or a copy of applicable safeguards.
