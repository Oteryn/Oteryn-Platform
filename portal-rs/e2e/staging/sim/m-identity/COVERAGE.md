# Identity and notifications route coverage

The source baseline is `7847cf7de91957d4317e7693d31d049b8f1c5f8e`. `P` expands to the empty prefix, `/pl`, and `/en`. `L` expands to `/pl` and `/en`. Routes registered with `{lang}` use those two supported locales. The tables list explicit methods.

The public web router mounts registration, verification, recovery keys, optional consents, and the OAuth browser and token routers. The binary composes the other browser adapters. The notify crate has no HTTP router of its own. The identity lifecycle adapter exposes its preferences and unsubscribe routes. ## Actor results

The account rows use the browser session's own identity. Staff do not receive an alternate owner permission in these routers. Invalid or active account state and possession of the relevant email capability, recovery key, or browser session determine access. The staff scenario assigns one distinct seeded role to each project.
